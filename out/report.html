Worst case does not fit: the extremes run -0.080 to 0.380 mm against a 0.05 to 0.25 mm specification. That alone is not a reason to change the design — the probability of every part sitting at its limit together is negligible — but it does mean the stack has to be justified statistically rather than by inspection.
Monte Carlo disagrees with RSS. RSS predicts 13,461 ppm, Monte Carlo 19,860 ppm — worse by 6,399 ppm. RSS assumes every contributor is normal and centred; the stack declares a skewed bearing width, a uniform shim and a mean shift on the bore, and that is the whole of the difference.
